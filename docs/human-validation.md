# Human validation

Automated output can be incomplete or wrong. A human must review claims and outcomes in proportion to their impact. A check, test, or model response is evidence about a limited scope; it is not a general guarantee.

## When review is required

Require human review before relying on output for consequential decisions, before executing destructive or externally visible actions, and before publishing claims about safety, privacy, or compatibility. Review documentation changes that establish policy or describe product capabilities for accuracy and clarity.

## Review record

For material changes or reports, record in concise terms:

- what was reviewed and the scope of review;
- what evidence or sources support the conclusion;
- what was not checked and any important uncertainty;
- what actions were taken, by whom or by what system, and their outcome;
- any remaining risks, limitations, or follow-up needed.

Do not imply that a human reviewed work if they did not. Keep review notes free of secrets and unnecessary personal information. For user-facing reports, separate facts, interpretations, recommendations, and action status. Use plain language, meaningful headings, and descriptive links; do not rely only on color, emoji, or persona tone to communicate warnings or status.
