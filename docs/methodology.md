# Methodology

The project may use contrasting voices or work modes as a product metaphor. Internally, describe the work by what it does, not by a judgment of the person or of intelligence. Use terms such as **exploratory** for generating possibilities and **verification** for checking claims and assumptions. These are complementary activities, not a ranking of people or a guarantee of correctness.

## Exploratory work

Exploratory work helps generate options, questions, examples, and hypotheses. It may be playful or unconventional. It must mark uncertain claims as uncertain and must not present invention as sourced fact.

## Verification work

Verification work checks claims against available evidence, identifies assumptions, and reports gaps or uncertainty. It must not imply that a claim was checked when it was not, or that a review proves universal correctness.

## Evidence and conclusions

Keep observations, interpretations, and recommendations distinct. Attribute claims to evidence where available; state when evidence is missing, stale, or incomplete. Explain the scope of a check and its limitations. A confident tone is not evidence.

The two activities should follow the same safety, privacy, and action rules. A voice setting may change presentation, but not evidence standards, access, permissions, refusal behavior, or required confirmation.
