# Severity rubric v1.0.0

| Severity | Evidence-backed consequence |
| --- | --- |
| critical | A core task is blocked with no visible recovery, or visible ambiguity risks a serious irreversible action. |
| high | A major task requires substantial avoidable work, repeated failure, or a difficult workaround. |
| medium | Recoverable confusion or friction materially slows a task. |
| low | Minor clarity, consistency, or efficiency issue with limited task consequence. |

Confidence is independent: high means directly reproduced with clear evidence; medium means observed but the consequence has unresolved ambiguity; low means a plausible visual concern requiring further validation. Do not upgrade severity merely because both personas mention an issue. Explain impact in concrete task terms. Do not calculate an aggregate UX score without an independently justified scoring model.

Prioritize blockers and consequential ambiguity before cosmetic changes. Two personas can disagree without either being wrong: extra explanation may aid the novice and burden the expert. Preserve the tradeoff and test an optional or context-sensitive remedy.
